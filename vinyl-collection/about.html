About

A home for the records, sorted by genre and styled to each artist's era.

How it's organised

Every record is filed under a genre — Pop, K-Pop, Rap/Hip-Hop, R&B/Soul, Rock, and Alt/Emo. Open any artist to see their records on a background themed to their era, from 80s neon and synthwave to after-dark red and Motown gold.

The vinyl-color trick

Hover an album cover and the record slides out, spins, and tells you the color of that pressing — orange, lavender split, seafoam, silver, red splatter, even an iridescent hologram. Standard pressings show classic black.
